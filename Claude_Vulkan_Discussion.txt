The validation error is telling you exactly what's wrong: you have a resolve attachment, but your color attachment has VK_SAMPLE_COUNT_1_BIT. Resolve attachments only make sense when you're downsampling from a higher sample count — they're the destination that MSAA writes its averaged result into. If your color attachment is already 1 sample, there's nothing to resolve from.
You have two ways to fix it depending on what you actually want:
Option A — You want MSAA (the intended path):
Change the sample count to something real:
cppVkSampleCountFlagBits msaaSamples = F_VulkanManager->GetMaxSamples();
// or a fixed value like VK_SAMPLE_COUNT_4_BIT if you don't want max
renderPass.SetMSAASampleCount(msaaSamples);
Then make sure your color attachment also uses that sample count:
cppcolorAttachment.samples = msaaSamples; // not VK_SAMPLE_COUNT_1_BIT
The resolve attachment stays at VK_SAMPLE_COUNT_1_BIT — that's correct, it's the final resolved output.
Option B — You don't want MSAA right now:
Don't call EnableMsaa() and remove the resolve attachment entirely from the render pass setup. The subpass pResolveAttachments should be nullptr. This is the simpler path while you're still building out the engine and eliminates the error immediately.
The same issue exists in CreatePostProcessingRenderPassResources() and CreateImGuiRendePassResources() — same hardcoded VK_SAMPLE_COUNT_1_BIT after calling EnableMsaa(). Fix all three consistently whichever way you go.
